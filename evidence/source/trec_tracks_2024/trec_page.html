The Text Retrieval Conference

... encouraging research in information retrieval from large text collections.

What is TREC?

An evaluation workshop series for measuring the effectiveness of search algorithms and other technologies that help us find information.

Who is TREC?

TREC is a community of researchers in information retrieval and natural language processing convened by the National Institute of Standards and Technology.

TREC has data.

Over more than three decades, TREC has produced dozens of datasets and developed measurement processes and tools for them. You can have it all.
