P46: 10. v4语义与Admission规则（保持不变）
--T8 (7x3)--
R: 任务 | 核心判定 | 正式Gold最低要求
R: Preference | 未来持续性 vs 本次约束 | scope/key/value/evidence/status；普通请求不可长期化
R: Retrieval | Query绑定frozen KB/qrel | relevant_ids/version/user/status/scope可复现
R: Conflict | 先判能否同时成立 | left/right evidence + conflict_basis + time/version
R: Forgetting | 删对且不误删 | inventory + target_ids + must_keep + checkpoints
R: Tool Result | 真实Runtime | trace + actual status + side-effect evidence
R: E2E | 真实多step场景 | steps + memory state + response + checkpoints + trace
--T9 (6x3)--
R: Admission | PASS标准 | 阈值
R: G1 Provenance | raw/source/license/version/locator或generation manifest完整 | unresolved=0
R: G2 Semantic | 符合任务矩阵或明确合法负例 | 人工100%确认
R: G3 Annotatable | 只看context+evidence即可独立判断 | ambiguous=0
R: G4 Diversity | 无exact dup；near已审；template/source不过度集中 | similarity>0.85送审；单template>25% FAIL
R: G5 Leakage | ID/content/evidence/raw/template无泄漏 | unresolved leak=0
